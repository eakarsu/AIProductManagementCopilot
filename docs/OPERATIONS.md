# Governed product management journey

The supported API is `/api/governed-product`. A versioned workspace graph durably links product areas, outcomes, discovery opportunities, experiments, roadmap items, releases, decisions, and owners. Evidence ingest is typed and source-digested for feedback, support, analytics, CRM, research, design, issue, delivery, and documentation records. Provider identity/version conflicts are retained for resolution; normalized feedback is deduplicated with a product-scoped fingerprint.

Opportunity scoring is deterministic and records reach/impact/effort assumptions, evidence IDs, staleness, missing signals, disagreement, and confidence. Every score is immutable history, so changing priorities can be reconstructed instead of overwritten. Decision forecasts are compared with authoritative adoption, retention, revenue, quality, and customer-result outcomes.

Organization/product membership is rechecked per request. Researchers, product managers, approvers, delivery operators, and auditors are separated; creators cannot self-approve. Confidential roadmap/customer evidence requires explicit access, and confidential publication requires a separate permission. Publication and ticket creation use provider-specific idempotent outboxes, retries, dead letters, and reconciled receipts. Export and retention-delete requests store a subject digest, require independent review, and never delete automatically.

Feedback, support, analytics, CRM, issue-tracker, design, documentation, and delivery providers fail closed until HTTPS endpoints and runtime credentials exist. The development seed requires `SEED_ACK=seed-local-pm-demo` plus a runtime-only `DEMO_SEED_PASSWORD` of at least 12 characters and is always refused in production. Startup never installs, creates/starts PostgreSQL, creates/migrates/seeds a database, generates secrets, or kills ports. Real provider contracts/mappings, retention policy execution, customer consent/privacy review, security tests, migration/restore rehearsal, outcome windows, and production approval remain external launch gates.
